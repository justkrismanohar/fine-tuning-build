# GPU question card — T1-04

Do not memorise SKUs. Two “24 GB” cards are not interchangeable. VRAM is necessary and not sufficient.

1. Is this box for a **burst** (fine-tune, then stop) or for **standing inference**, and for how many users?
2. How much VRAM, at what **precision**?
3. Architecture / **compute capability** — will vLLM actually run?
4. Is the job compute-bound or memory-bandwidth-bound?
5. Consumer vs datacentre (ECC, multi-user, driver, licence)?
6. Can it **idle-stop**?
