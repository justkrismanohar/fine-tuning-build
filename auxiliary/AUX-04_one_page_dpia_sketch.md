# AUX-04 A one-page DPIA sketch

**Self-taught. 40 minutes. No GPU. Not legal advice. Not a filed DPIA.**

The contact hours give you the questions. They do not file a Data Protection Act assessment.

## Who this is for

Every PII-heavy paper: Labour CVs, Health LMS and drafts, MPSDFS cases, THA health, RDLG burgess files, Trade tickets, TTDF ops (usually: **do not process**).

## The page

Copy this and fill it for the *type* of problem on your canvas. Use CivicDesk words, not real people.

1. **Purpose.** One sentence. If you cannot say it, you cannot collect.
2. **Lawful basis / authority.** Who in the ministry is allowed to run this process today, without AI.
3. **Data.** Categories only (e.g. “fictional applicant education fields”). No samples from work.
4. **What the model sees.** Prompt only? Retrieved chunks? A fine-tune set? Default is: as little as possible; never production CVs in this course.
5. **Where it runs.** Laptop / Ministry VM / MEA Azure / public API. Cross-border?
6. **Who can see the output.** Role, not name.
7. **Retention.** Chat log, ticket, evaluation set — three different clocks.
8. **Human.** Who can override, and where that is recorded (N14).
9. **Failure.** What happens if the model is wrong in the harmful direction.
10. **Decision.** Proceed to a *project* request for MPAAI / legal, or stop.

## Stop

A filled sketch is a transfer-card attachment. It is not approval to process personal data.
